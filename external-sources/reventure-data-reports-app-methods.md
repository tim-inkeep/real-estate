# Reventure Data Reports and App Source Methods

Source note for downstream research. Full source URLs are preserved in frontmatter and below.

## Captured source facts

- Reventure Consulting describes its data reports as free U.S. housing-market reports covering home prices, inventory, and seller price cuts by city.
- Reventure says the reports are updated monthly from sources including Zillow, Realtor.com, and the U.S. Census.
- Reventure states that data is available down to ZIP Code level.
- The Reventure Metro Data Dashboard page lists Realtor.com Residential Listings Database as a source.
- The Reventure Inventory Dashboard page also lists Realtor.com Residential Listings Database as a source.
- The Reventure Building Permits Dashboard page lists the U.S. Census Bureau as its permit data source.
