# House Price Appreciation by State and Metro Area: Fourth Quarter 2025

**Author:** Jing Fu — **Published:** March 4, 2026

U.S. housing prices decelerated in Q4 2025, growing at the slowest pace since mid-2012. According to the Federal Housing Finance Agency's purchase-only House Price Index, national appreciation reached 1.8% in the fourth quarter of 2025 compared to the same period in 2024, with quarterly gains of just 0.8%.

## State-Level Performance

Of 50 states plus D.C., 43 states posted positive annual appreciation ranging from 0.1% to 6.4%. Top performers included North Dakota (6.4%), Delaware (6.3%), and Illinois (6.1%). Nine states and D.C. experienced declines, with Florida suffering the steepest drop at -2.7%.

## Metropolitan Area Trends

Among the 100 largest metros, appreciation ranged from -9.1% to +8.9%. Cape Coral-Fort Myers, Florida posted the worst performance, while Allentown-Bethlehem-Easton, PA–NJ led gains. Of 100 metros tracked, 66 showed appreciation while 34 experienced declines.

## Regional Patterns

Many of the strongest performers were concentrated in the Midwest and Northeast, with limited inventory and lower price points, contrasting with Sun Belt and Mountain West areas facing affordability pressures after earlier appreciation surges.
