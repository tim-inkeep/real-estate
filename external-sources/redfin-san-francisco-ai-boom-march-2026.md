# Redfin Reports San Francisco Home Prices Jump Most in 8 Years Amid AI Boom

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: April 16, 2026.
- Redfin reports San Francisco metro median sale price rose 14.4% year over year in March 2026 to $1.7 million, the largest increase among the 50 most populous metros.
- Redfin reports San Francisco condo prices rose 24.4% year over year.
- Redfin attributes market heat to AI-industry growth, return-to-office dynamics, and lack of inventory.
- Redfin reports the typical San Francisco home sold for 8.9% above final list price in March 2026, while the typical U.S. home sold 1.3% below final list price.
- Nationally, Redfin reports median U.S. sale price up 1.2% year over year in March 2026, pending sales down 2.6% year over year, active listings up 0.5% year over year, and 55 median days on market.
