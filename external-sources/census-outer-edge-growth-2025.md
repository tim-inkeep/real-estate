# Census Movin' Out: More Growth on Outer Edges of Major Cities

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source context: May 2026.
- Census reports Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington metro population at 8.5 million in 2025, up 11.0% from April 2020.
- Census reports Celina, Texas, at 64,427 residents and up 276.8% from 2020.
- Census says the Dallas metro gained about 270,000 residents through net domestic migration from 2020 to 2025, roughly 40% more than any other metro, with much of the growth farthest from the center.
- Census contrasts Dallas-Fort Worth outer-edge growth with New York-Newark-Jersey City, which was up 0.1% from April 2020 and had fewer large-growth incorporated places.
