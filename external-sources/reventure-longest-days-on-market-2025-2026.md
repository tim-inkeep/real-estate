## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: October 21, 2025.
- Reventure identifies long days on market and price cuts as signs of weaker buyer urgency heading into 2026.
- Metro details reported include Dallas at 63 days, -3.8% prices, 33.6% seller cuts, and 30,064 homes of inventory.
- Tampa is reported at 79 days, -6.3% prices, 32% seller cuts, and 18,674 homes of inventory.
- Phoenix is reported at 65 days, -3.6% values, 34% seller cuts, and 17,070 homes of inventory.
- Chicago is reported as steadier: 38 days, +3.4% prices, 21.9% cuts, and a 3.6 value-to-income ratio.
- Reventure's conclusion says buyers have more choices but are taking longer, with Chicago and Houston potentially stabilizing before more expensive metros.
