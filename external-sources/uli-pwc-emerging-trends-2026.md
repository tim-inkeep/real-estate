# ULI/PwC Emerging Trends in Real Estate 2026 Press Release

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: November 5, 2025.
- ULI/PwC says the report draws on more than 1,700 investors, developers, lenders, and advisors across the U.S. and Canada.
- Top 10 markets to watch in 2026 listed by ULI/PwC: Dallas-Fort Worth, Jersey City, Miami, Brooklyn, Houston, Nashville, Northern New Jersey, Tampa-St. Petersburg, Manhattan, and Phoenix.
- The source highlights data centers, senior housing, and self-storage as sectors with growth or resilience.
- The source says the office market is bifurcating, with top-tier major-market buildings capturing rents while lower-quality and less central properties struggle.
- ULI/PwC frames 2026 real estate growth around innovation, adaptation, efficiency, and strategic reinvention rather than a broad return to old patterns.
