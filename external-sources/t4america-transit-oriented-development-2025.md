# Unlocking the Benefits of Transit-Oriented Development

**Author:** Elisa Ramirez (Transportation for America) — **Published:** June 13, 2025

## Transit-Oriented Development & Value Capture

Transportation for America's proposal aims to encourage transit-oriented development (TOD) through federal funding priorities — prioritizing Capital Investment Grants projects that include value-capture mechanisms and support mixed-use, mixed-income development around stations.

## Real Estate Performance

"Transit projects increase nearby property values by 30 to 40 percent." This value creation represents a significant but largely uncaptured public benefit.

The Orange Line corridor in Arlington County, Virginia demonstrates the outcome: the two-square-mile corridor now contains "26 percent of the county's residents and 37 percent of the county's jobs" despite representing just "8 percent of the county's land area." Additionally, "50 percent of residents" use transit for commuting and "73 percent of residents" walk to metro stations.

## Value Capture

Value capture recovers unearned profits accruing to private landowners from public transit investments. Joint development strategies allow transit agencies to coordinate with developers, creating revenue streams for transit operations.

## Housing & Zoning

The article emphasizes that outdated single-family zoning restrictions undercut transit efficiency; federal programs should reward localities removing these restrictions to meet housing demand in walkable, transit-served areas.
