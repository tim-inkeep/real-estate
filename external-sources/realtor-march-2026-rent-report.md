# Realtor.com March 2026 Rental Report

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source context: March 2026 report, fetched May 22, 2026.
- Realtor.com reports March 2026 as the 32nd consecutive month of year-over-year rent declines for 0-2 bedroom properties across the 50 largest metros.
- Realtor.com reports median asking rent at $1,669, down 1.5% year over year, down 5.4% from the summer 2022 peak, and up 17.5% from pre-pandemic levels.
- Realtor.com reports renting a starter home was cheaper than buying in all 50 largest metros, with an average monthly savings of $920.
- Realtor.com's top rent-favoring markets include Austin, Seattle, Phoenix, Los Angeles, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Francisco, Sacramento, Nashville, Columbus, and San Jose.
- Realtor.com reports Austin rent at $1,361 and buying cost at $3,080, a 126.3% buy-rent difference; Phoenix rent at $1,435 and buy cost at $2,627; Dallas rent at $1,461 and buy cost at $2,639.
