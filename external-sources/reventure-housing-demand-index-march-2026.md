# Reventure Housing Demand Index Increases to 11/100 in March 2026

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: March 18, 2026.
- Reventure reports its Housing Demand Index at 11/100 in mid-March 2026, up from 8/100 the prior week.
- Reventure describes the index as a forward-looking demand indicator combining pending sales, mortgage applications, internet home-search activity, and buyer sentiment.
- Reventure states buyer demand was down more than 30% from pre-pandemic norms and that its three-month moving average was 8/100.
- Reventure cites University of Michigan sentiment data saying roughly 78% of Americans viewed it as a bad time to buy a home.
- Reventure's March 2026 forecast called for national home prices to change by about 0.2% through February 2027, while noting large local variation.

## Use in research

Use this as the Reventure anchor for weak national demand, transaction-volume pressure, and the need to separate national headline weakness from local metro divergence.
