# US Housing Market Entering 'New Era' in 2026

**By Giulia Carbonaro, Senior Housing Reporter** — **Published:** Dec 01, 2025, 04:43 AM EST

## Key Regional Divide

According to real estate analyst Nick Gerli, CEO of Reventure App, the U.S. housing market faces a sharp bifurcation in 2026:

**Appreciating markets (Rust Belt):**
- Cleveland, Hartford, Albany, Chicago
- Tight inventory maintaining price growth
- Mortgage Cost/Income ratios around 30 percent (up from 20 percent pre-pandemic)
- More sustainable demand from local buyers who can qualify for mortgages

**Declining markets (Sun Belt):**
- Florida, Texas, Arizona
- Decade-high inventory levels
- Price declines despite the national average rising
- Florida median home price: $408,400 (down 0.39% year-over-year, October 2025)
- Texas median home price: $341,800 (down 0.81% year-over-year, October 2025)
- Mortgage Cost/Income ratios over 35 percent (up from below 25 percent in October 2019)

## The Pandemic Reversal

Sun Belt cities like Austin and Nashville experienced explosive growth from 2020–2022 when remote work enabled relocation. Return-to-office mandates and rising mortgage rates subsequently reversed migration patterns, leaving excess inventory and eroding buyer demand in these formerly hot markets.

Gerli predicts this regional divergence will solidify and persist for years ahead.
