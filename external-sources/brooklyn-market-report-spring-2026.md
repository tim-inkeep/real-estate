# Brooklyn Real Estate Market Report: Spring 2026

**Published:** April 21, 2026

Borough-wide prices climbed 4.2% year-over-year through April 2026; "the setup still tilts toward sellers but no longer punishes patient buyers."

## Key Metrics (April 2026)

- Median sale price: $850,000 (+4.2% YoY)
- Median price per sq ft: $1,074 (+13.4% YoY)
- Days on market: 55 (down 13 days YoY)
- Months of supply: 3.8 (a seller's market; balanced = 6 months)
- 30-year fixed rate: 6.09% (down from 6.83%)

## Price by Property Type

- Single-family homes: $950,000 median (+5.1% YoY)
- Two-family homes: $1,200,000 median (+6.2% YoY)
- Condos: $1,100,000 median (+2.8% YoY)
- Co-ops: $460,000 median (−1.3% YoY)

## Days on Market by Segment

- Single-family under $1M in southern Brooklyn: 18–28 days
- Mid-tier condos ($900K–$1.4M): 60–75 days
- Co-ops above $700K: 90+ days

## Winners and Losers

- **Winners:** single-family detached and two-family homes, especially southern Brooklyn (multiple offers on well-priced listings).
- **Split:** condos divided — Manhattan-adjacent luxury (DUMBO, Brooklyn Heights) strong; mid-tier condos in Crown Heights, Bed-Stuy, Williamsburg softened with rising inventory and concessions.
- **Loser:** co-ops down 1.3% YoY. Active listings ran ~14% above April 2025, gains concentrated in co-ops and mid-tier condos; southern Brooklyn single-family under $900K remains tight.
