# NAR 2026 Housing Hot Spots

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source metadata/search result: December 2025.
- NAR frames 2026 opportunity around markets where inventory returns, listed prices align better with local incomes, and lower mortgage rates expand the qualified buyer pool.
- NAR identifies Charleston, Charlotte, Columbus, Indianapolis, Jacksonville, Minneapolis-St. Paul, Raleigh, Richmond, Salt Lake City, and Spokane as 2026 housing hot spots.
- NAR's report says a decline in rates from 7% to 6% can expand the qualified buyer pool by 5.5 million households, including 1.6 million renters.
- NAR projects existing-home sales to increase by 14% in 2026 and home prices to increase by about 4% nationally.

## Use in research

Use this for the 'returning transaction opportunity' lens: metros with demand potential and returning inventory can be good operating markets even when they are not the tightest seller markets.
