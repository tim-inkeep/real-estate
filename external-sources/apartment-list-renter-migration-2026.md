# Apartment List Renter Migration Report 2026

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: March 19, 2026.
- Apartment List analyzes registered user search preferences from January 1 through December 31, 2025.
- Apartment List says renter search data and Census data signal sustained interest in lower-density, lower-cost regions, especially the Southeast and Mountain West.
- Apartment List reports Savannah, Durham, and Charleston as top metros where more than 60% of apartment searches come from out-of-market renters.
- Apartment List identifies search channels from Salt Lake City into Ogden, Sacramento/San Francisco into Reno, and Denver into Colorado Springs.
- Apartment List says long-term affordability in these growing rental markets depends on whether enough supply is available to absorb new renters.

## Use in research

Use this for renter-demand urban-form conclusions: smaller metros and secondary nodes can attract out-of-market renters, but supply absorption determines pricing power.
