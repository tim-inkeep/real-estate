# San Jose Rental Market Data (September 2025)

- **Average rent:** $3,500/month as of September 2025 (apartments average 704 sq ft); roughly double the national median (~$1,535).
- **Year-over-year change:** +3.7% (about $124 more per month).
- **By size:** Studio $2,238 (494 sq ft); 1-bedroom $2,566; 2-bedroom $3,260; 3-bedroom $3,973+.
- **Highest-rent areas:** Sun Neighborhood ($4,048), River Oaks, Santana Row. **Most affordable:** East San Jose ($2,067–$2,800), Blackford, Strawberry Park.

## Market Context

Median home price reported at $1,475,000, with typical sales occurring within 12 days. San Jose's position as Silicon Valley's core drives demand; the city's population reached 1.821 million in 2023.
