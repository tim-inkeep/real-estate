# Census Population Growth Holds Steady in Midsized Cities Amid Slowdown

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: May 14, 2026.
- Census reports that big-city growth slowed significantly between 2024 and 2025, while midsized cities found stronger growth patterns through migration and new housing.
- Census says Charlotte gained more residents than any city from 2024 to 2025 but was outpaced by several midsized cities in its own metro by percentage growth, including Fort Mill, South Carolina.
- Census reports the five fastest-growing cities over 20,000 people were all in Texas, four clustered in Dallas-Fort Worth suburbs and one outside Houston.
- Census reports Austin crossed 1 million residents and Raleigh crossed 500,000 between 2024 and 2025.
- Census reports national housing stock reached 148.3 million units in 2025, up 1.4 million or 1.0% from 2024.
