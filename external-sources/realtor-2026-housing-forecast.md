## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: December 3, 2025.

- Realtor.com forecasts 2026 home-price growth of 2.2%, rent decline of 1.0%, existing-home inventory growth of 8.9%, single-family new-home starts growth of 3.1%, and home-sales growth of 1.7% to 4.13 million.
