# San Francisco Real Estate Forecast 2026: The AI Boom & Market Trends

**Published:** January 21, 2026 — **Author:** Mark D McHale

San Francisco has rebounded from its 2022–2023 dip (prices softened ~13%); luxury District 5 (Pacific Heights, Cow Hollow) hit new all-time peaks by late 2025.

## AI Boom Driving Residential Demand

The primary market driver is artificial-intelligence expansion. AI firms absorbed over 1 million square feet of office space in Q3 2025 alone (CoStar / CRE Daily). Anthropic and OpenAI are fueling a return-to-office movement and residential demand as employees seek proximity to "Cerebral Valley" (Hayes Valley). Mission Bay is an emerging luxury condo market; Pacific Heights and Cow Hollow hit all-time highs.

## Rates, Rent, Supply

- 30-year fixed rate ~6.06% (January 2026); projected ~5.9% by year-end if inflation cools.
- Rental growth: +5.9% year-over-year (Q4 2025 Matthews report) — San Francisco "leading the nation in rent growth."
- Pending home sales (late 2025): +17% year-over-year.
- Median home price: $1.5 million+; the market had ~1.8 months of supply.
- To meet California's mandate of 82,069 new units by 2031, Mayor Daniel Lurie signed the "Family Zoning Plan" in December 2025, enabling up to four units on most lots in the Richmond and Sunset districts.

## Market Bifurcation

Colliers analysts describe a "bifurcated" market where AI-driven sectors and luxury districts expand while traditional industries lag. High-rise luxury condos in South Beach and Mission Bay are positioned as entry points for buyers priced out of single-family homes. Median prices exceeding $1.5 million exclude vast segments of the population — a persistent "Tech Wealth Gap."
