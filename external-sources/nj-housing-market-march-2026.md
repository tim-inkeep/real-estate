# New Jersey Housing Market Report: March 2026

**Published:** March 18, 2026

The statewide median home price reached $531,000 in February 2026, a 4.8% increase from $506,500 one year earlier. Single-family homes posted a $592,000 median; condos and townhouses averaged $415,000.

**Statewide key metrics:** Active inventory 25,100 homes (+11.2% YoY), ~3.2 months of supply; days on market 55 (down from 58 in January); 30-year fixed mortgage rates averaging 6.1%; 7,400 closed sales in February (+6.3% YoY).

## County-by-County

**Hudson County**
- Median price: $560,000 (condos: $485,000)
- Active listings: 1,480
- Days on market: 52
- Average rent: $3,150/month (+3.3% YoY)
- Jersey City neighborhoods (Downtown, Paulus Hook, Hamilton Park) "see properties move in under 30 days when priced correctly." Condo-dominant market reflecting the urban vertical composition.
- Hoboken noted as a major market driver, with strong PATH train access to Manhattan sustaining "intense" rental demand in waterfront neighborhoods.

**Bergen County** — median $742,000 (single-family $835,000); 1,720 listings (1.6 months supply); 42 days; rent $2,830/mo (+4.1% YoY).

**Middlesex County** — median $528,000; 1,850 listings (3.4 months); 48 days; rent $2,570/mo.

**Monmouth County** — median $585,000; 2,100 listings (+12.8%); 50 days; rent $2,650/mo.

**Essex County** — median $495,000; 2,250 listings (+14.2%, highest increase); 57 days; rent $2,380/mo.

## Pricing & Appreciation

Year-over-year appreciation by price tier: entry-level (under $400K) +5.6%; mid-range ($400K–$700K) +4.3%; upper-market ($700K–$1M) +3.7%; luxury (above $1M) +2.9%. Price growth is moderating from the 5.4% annual rate recorded at year-end 2025.

## Rental Market & Outlook

Statewide average rents reached $2,480/month (+3.6% YoY); vacancy near 4.2%, tight relative to the 5–6% balanced threshold. The report forecasts prices rising 3–5% annually through 2026; inventory expected to peak in June–July at 28,000–30,000 active listings; mortgage rates projected to drift toward 5.8–6.0% by year-end 2026.
