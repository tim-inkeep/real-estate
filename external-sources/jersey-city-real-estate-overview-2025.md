# Jersey City Real Estate Market Overview (Q3 2025 data)

As of Q3 / October 2025:

- **Median sale price:** $657,300 — a 3.2% rise year-over-year
- **Median list price:** ~$699K — up 3.6% year-over-year
- **Median sale price per square foot:** ~$548 — up 12.1% year-over-year
- **Days on market:** ~61 days; properties received ~2 offers on average
- Transaction count showed a notable ~70% year-over-year decline (low sales volume)
- 5+ bedroom homes saw sale prices up 33.4% year-over-year

## Market Framing

Jersey City is described as an influential market driver within Hudson County, with its median listing price notably above the county average. As part of the New York metro area, "the convenience of travel to Manhattan makes Jersey City an attractive locale for those working in New York but seeking more favorable real estate prices" — competing as an alternative to the New York City market while influenced by the same economic forces. New home construction is noted as adding to overall housing supply.
