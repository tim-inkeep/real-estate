# National Association of Realtors Unveils 10 Top Housing Hot Spots for 2025

**Published:** December 12, 2024

NAR announced its forecast for the 2025 housing market, projecting 4.5 million existing-home sales and a median price of $410,700. NAR Chief Economist Lawrence Yun stated that "important factors common among top performing markets include available inventory at affordable price points" and net migration patterns.

## The 10 Top Housing Hot Spots for 2025

1. Boston-Cambridge-Newton, Massachusetts–New Hampshire
2. Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia, North Carolina–South Carolina
3. Grand Rapids-Kentwood, Michigan
4. Greenville-Anderson, South Carolina
5. Hartford-East Hartford-Middletown, Connecticut
6. Indianapolis-Carmel-Anderson, Indiana
7. Kansas City, Missouri–Kansas
8. Knoxville, Tennessee
9. Phoenix-Mesa-Chandler, Arizona
10. San Antonio-New Braunfels, Texas

The South region leads with four markets; the Midwest contains three.

## Key 2025 Forecasts

- Mortgage rates expected to stabilize near 6%
- Home price appreciation projected at approximately 2%
- Housing starts anticipated to reach 1.45 million units

## NAR's 10 Evaluation Criteria

Markets were assessed using these indicators: share of locked-in homeowners, average mortgage rates, job growth, millennial renter affordability, net migration ratios, households reaching homebuying age, out-of-state movers, homeowner tenure, starter-owner units, and price appreciation.
