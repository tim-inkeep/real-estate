# Zillow 10 Hottest Housing Markets for 2026

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: April 19, 2026.
- Zillow ranks Hartford, Buffalo, New York, Providence, San Jose, Philadelphia, Boston, Los Angeles, Richmond, and Milwaukee as its top 10 hottest for-sale markets for 2026.
- Zillow's methodology uses home-price growth, sale speed, price-cut share, above-list share, and new jobs relative to permits.
- Zillow reports Hartford had 63% fewer homes for sale than pre-pandemic, 66% of 2025 sales above asking, 16.5% of listings with price cuts, and a 3.9% forecast home-value rise in 2026.
- Zillow reports Providence had 55% fewer homes for sale than pre-pandemic and a 3.0% forecast home-value rise in 2026.
- Zillow reports Milwaukee had 26% fewer homes for sale than pre-pandemic, 50% of 2025 sales above asking, and a 2.1% forecast home-value rise in 2026.
