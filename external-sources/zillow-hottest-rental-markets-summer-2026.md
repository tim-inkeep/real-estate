# Providence Tops Zillow's Hottest Rental Markets for Summer 2026

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: May 18, 2026.
- Zillow ranks Providence, New York, San Francisco, Hartford, Los Angeles, Chicago, Boston, Milwaukee, Virginia Beach, and San Jose as the top 10 hottest rental markets for summer 2026.
- Zillow says demand exceeds available rental homes in the hottest markets and that 2024's high national construction volume largely missed the Northeast and coastal California.
- Top-10 data reported by Zillow includes Providence at 5.0% annual rent growth, 12.9% concession share, and a 5.1% vacancy forecast.
- Zillow reports New York at 4.5% rent growth and a 4.3% vacancy forecast; San Francisco at 5.4% rent growth and 4.3% vacancy; Chicago at 5.7% rent growth; Milwaukee at 4.1% rent growth.
- Zillow contrasts these markets with Austin, Tampa, and Phoenix, where new rental construction has kept rent growth in check.

## Use in research

Use this as the main 2026 rental-demand source for constrained legacy/coastal metros and as a contrast with oversupplied Sun Belt rental markets.
