# Reventure's 2025 Housing Forecast Review: A Look Back at Our Performance

**Author:** Nick (Reventure App) — **Published:** December 2, 2025

## Executive Summary

Reventure App claims to have delivered "the #1 U.S. housing forecast of 2025," outperforming major competitors including Zillow, Redfin, CoreLogic, and Goldman Sachs.

## National-Level Performance

Reventure forecasted U.S. home values would grow 0.9% in 2025. The actual outcome was 0.06% growth, putting their estimate within 0.85 percentage points of reality. Competitors overshot by an average of 2.57%, nearly three times Reventure's error rate.

## State-Level Results

**Strong performers (as forecasted):** Connecticut, New Jersey, Rhode Island, Illinois

**Weak performers (as forecasted):** Florida, Texas, Colorado, Washington DC

The median state-level error was -1.90%. Reventure missed on Vermont and Maine, where it predicted 5–6% growth but values declined due to limited-inventory dynamics differing from national patterns.

## Metro-Level Accuracy

Across the 109 largest U.S. metros (500,000+ population), Reventure reported a 76% correlation coefficient — "six times higher than Zillow's forecast accuracy."

**Correctly identified outperformers:** Hartford, Bridgeport, Albany, Syracuse, Cleveland

**Correctly identified decliners:** Cape Coral, North Port, Tampa, Austin, Lakeland, Orlando, Dallas, San Antonio, Denver, Colorado Springs, Memphis, Nashville

Median metro-level error was +2.50%, attributed partly to "unexpectedly sharp weakening in several Sun Belt metros."

## ZIP-Code Performance

ZIP-code forecasts were less accurate, with correlations 30–40% lower than broader geographic scales due to smaller transaction volumes and volatility. Reventure reported it correctly identified neighborhood-level disparities in Atlanta, Dallas, and Phoenix.
