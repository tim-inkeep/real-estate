# Los Angeles Housing Forecast 2026: Home Prices, Rates & Micro-Markets

**Published:** December 12 — **Author:** Rinde Philippe

The Los Angeles housing market is entering a normalization phase in 2026 — stability and analytical buyer behavior rather than volatility.

## Price Outlook

LA home prices are "expected to remain largely flat to modestly positive in 2026, with most forecasts pointing to low single-digit appreciation, roughly in the 1%–4% range."

## Four Market Drivers

1. **Mortgage rates** — expected to settle near the low-6% range.
2. **Inventory** — rising but "structurally constrained" due to zoning restrictions, construction costs, and owners holding ultra-low rates.
3. **Operating costs** — insurance premiums and property-tax reassessments now significantly impact buyer affordability.
4. **Buyer analytics** — buyers compare price-per-square-foot, days on market, and closed-sale data.

## Neighborhood Forecasts (2026 projections)

Santa Monica +3% to +5%; Brentwood (90049) +2.5% to +4.5%; Beverly Hills +2% to +4%; Bel Air +1.5% to +3.5%; Culver City +3% to +5%; Venice +2.5% to +4.5%; Palms/Mar Vista +3% to +5%; West LA (90025) +2.5% to +4.5%; Westwood/Century City +3% to +5%.

## Outlook

"No data supporting a housing crash" — high homeowner equity, low forced-sale risk, chronic underbuilding. Los Angeles operates as "hundreds of micro-markets" where neighborhood-level analysis matters more than citywide averages. 2026 modestly favors buyers (slower price growth, more inventory choice); sellers must price to recent closed sales.
