# Boston Housing Market, March 2026: Prices, Sales, and Inventory

**Published:** April 19, 2026

The Boston housing market showed stronger activity in March 2026, with prices rising and sales picking up as the spring season began.

- **Median sale price (Redfin):** ~$867,500, up ~3.3% vs March 2025.
- **Median home value (Zillow Home Value Index):** $798,217, down ~0.4% YoY. (The gap reflects methodology — Redfin tracks completed transactions; Zillow blends automated valuations with listings and pending sales.)
- **Sales volume:** 332 homes (slightly higher than a year earlier).
- **Days on market:** 33 (Redfin); 26 days to pending (Zillow).
- **Active listings:** ~1,368 (city); 536 new listings at end of March.

Inventory remains a significant constraint. (Per related coverage, Boston had just 8,621 homes listed metro-wide in March 2026 versus 13,016 in March 2019, though inventory was up 19.7% year-over-year.) Boston remains significantly more expensive than the national average; demand continues despite elevated mortgage rates. "The question for 2026 is no longer when rates will drop to historic lows — they likely won't — but how buyers will compete in a landscape where inventory remains critically scarce."
