# 2026 Chicago Market Report: Prices Up, Rates Down, Inventory Low

## 2025 Market Results

- Median home prices increased ~6.5% year-over-year; single-family homes appreciated nearly 13%.
- Total inventory dropped ~13% vs 2024; single-family inventory fell over 20%; condo inventory also tightened.
- Average mortgage rates eased from 6.72% (2024) to ~6.3% (2025).
- Home sales increased despite reduced inventory; average days on market declined.
- Consumer confidence declined sharply in 2025 amid cost-of-living and economic uncertainty.

Price growth was driven by location, property type, and limited supply. Coverage area: Chicago's North Side (Lincoln Park, Lakeview, Logan Square, Humboldt Park).
