# Census 2025 Metro and County Population Estimates

Source note for downstream research. Full source URL is preserved in frontmatter.

## Captured source facts

- Publication date shown by source: March 26, 2026.
- Census reports 310 of 387 U.S. metro areas had slower population growth between 2024 and 2025 than in the prior year.
- Census attributes much of the slowdown to lower net international migration; nine out of ten counties had lower international migration than in the prior year.
- Top numeric-growth metros from July 2024 to July 2025: Houston (+126,720), Dallas-Fort Worth (+123,557), Atlanta (+61,953), Phoenix (+59,065), Charlotte (+54,122), Austin (+53,796), Washington DC (+50,206), Seattle (+43,068), San Antonio (+38,402), and Orlando (+37,690).
- Top percent-growth metros include Ocala (+3.4%), Myrtle Beach (+3.2%), Spartanburg (+2.8%), Lakeland-Winter Haven (+2.7%), Punta Gorda (+2.7%), Huntsville (+2.6%), Wilmington NC (+2.6%), and St. George (+2.5%).

## Use in research

Use this for demand-side population momentum and to distinguish high-population-growth Sun Belt/exurban markets from price/rent markets that may be oversupplied.
